Updated 2 October 2026 · Source reference: build 1.4.3.5, Head 4909002
Firing-table provenance and narrower mapping scopes are labeled separately. No effective vehicle simulator is claimed.

Reading the evidence

The vehicle research traces configuration, not a validated vehicle simulator. Health, weapon choice, station ownership, default loadout, material interaction and native damage are separate questions. Matching a field name does not close all of them.

Read a value together with its owner, build, descriptor and selection route. A familiar field name or an identical number on another object is not enough to transfer its meaning.

EvidenceWhat it establishesWhat it does not establish
Stored game valueAn exact field on the selected serialized owner.Does not by itself establish activation, effective units or the native operation.
Source mappingA field name or association qualified by its declaring class, descriptor key and member schema.Schema-based name transfer is distinct from an inherited value and from native execution.
Inherited / registry defaultA base-class value or a named registry leaf, identified separately in the text.Never silently replace or average with the selected export’s own value.
Game description / UI summaryThe named card or menu statement; summaries retain their summary label.Does not establish a gameplay measurement or an exact UI-to-gameplay asset join.
EA patch notesAn EA statement at the explicitly cited update version.Does not prove the saved game build still behaves that way.
InterpretationA proposed explanation, correspondence or hypothesis.Does not establish a native equation or validated gameplay effect.
Analyzer modelA rule or calculated output in the pinned Analyzer software.Source-backed inputs do not automatically validate the software equation as native behavior.
In-game observationThe specifically cited game capture or test.Its result retains its weapon, loadout, build and measurement scope.

The colors identify the kind of evidence, not a confidence score. Field names, values and interpretations retain the qualifications beside them.

Null, zero, false and −1 are different observations. Do not turn an absent boxed default into zero, interpret −1 as “infinite,” or add the members of an unnamed tuple. “No join found” describes the checked scope, not proof that a feature does not exist.
