Weapons

How weapon identities, firing owners, damage curves, recoil, spread and handling arrays become Analyzer inputs.

Updated 2 October 2026 · Source reference: build 1.4.3.5, Head 4909002
Current research is distinguished from historical controls and the pinned Analyzer’s software policies.

What this guide covers

A weapon does not have one authoritative “stats file.” Its blueprint selects firing and projectile owners; its GS asset supplies recoil, dispersion and indices; attachments select additional modifiers; material tables resolve damage relationships; the Analyzer then applies its own explicit model. This guide follows that chain and identifies where source evidence ends and software policy begins.

The scope is the 63 weapons supported by the pinned Analyzer snapshot. This is a mechanics and tracing reference, not a claim that every weapon has been re-tested. The wider source census contains 64 candidate GS/WB/Ability triples; KSG’s exact campaign-only roots are excluded from the site roster without excluding every shared asset in its folder.

Read a value together with its owner, build, descriptor and selection route. A familiar field name or an identical number on another object is not enough to transfer its meaning.

EvidenceWhat it establishesWhat it does not establish
Stored game valueAn exact field on the selected serialized owner.Does not by itself establish activation, effective units or the native operation.
Source mappingA field name or association qualified by its declaring class, descriptor key and member schema.Schema-based name transfer is distinct from an inherited value and from native execution.
Inherited / registry defaultA base-class value or a named registry leaf, identified separately in the text.Never silently replace or average with the selected export’s own value.
Game description / UI summaryThe named card or menu statement; summaries retain their summary label.Does not establish a gameplay measurement or an exact UI-to-gameplay asset join.
EA patch notesAn EA statement at the explicitly cited update version.Does not prove the saved game build still behaves that way.
InterpretationA proposed explanation, correspondence or hypothesis.Does not establish a native equation or validated gameplay effect.
Analyzer modelA rule or calculated output in the pinned Analyzer software.Source-backed inputs do not automatically validate the software equation as native behavior.
In-game observationThe specifically cited game capture or test.Its result retains its weapon, loadout, build and measurement scope.

The colors identify the kind of evidence, not a confidence score. Field names, values and interpretations retain the qualifications beside them.

Null, zero, false and −1 are different observations. Do not turn an absent boxed default into zero, interpret −1 as “infinite,” or add the members of an unnamed tuple. “No join found” describes the checked scope, not proof that a feature does not exist.

Damage curves and hit zones

Read the selected curve, not just StartDamage

Analyzer model The Analyzer stores an ordered dmg array of {r, d, source?} points. It interpolates between distinct ranges and clamps outside the recorded extent. Repeated ranges encode instantaneous tier changes: at the exact repeated range the earlier/outgoing value wins, and the next tier applies just beyond it.

D(r) = d0 + (d1 − d0) × (r − r0) / (r1 − r0)

M433 example in the site model
exactly 21 m:       26.05
immediately beyond: 20.67

Keep equal-range points in source order.
Do not collapse duplicate ranges or sort them by damage.

Source / capture comparison Native curve selection and source-to-game interpretation still need evidence. The site uses stepped curves for ordinary non-sniper/non-shotgun weapons. The M45A1 is an instructive exception to naive import: its raw curve spans 54 m / 14.3 to 75 m / 12.5, but the retained September 13 test supported a step at 75 m, so the site preserves a 75 m / 14.3 point.

Bullets to kill and firing time

Analyzer model TTK here is conditional arithmetic for the chosen hit sequence and loadout. It does not estimate reaction time, aiming skill, misses, armor, suppression or healing.

Bullets-to-kill policy

H = selected health (the UI uses 100)
D = damage per shot at the selected range
h = head multiplier; b = selected body-zone multiplier
n = nonnegative integer requested headshots

lethalHeadshots = ceil((H − 1e−9) / (D × h))
if n >= lethalHeadshots:
    BTK = lethalHeadshots
else:
    BTK = n + max(0, ceil((H − n × D × h − 1e−9) / (D × b)))

Analyzer model The tiny tolerance is a software choice for floating-point lethal thresholds. At 25 chest damage against 100 health, four hits are required. Display damage may be rounded or capped while the underlying damage remains uncapped.

Cadence, burst gaps and optional time additions

Normal interval = 60 / R
R = rounds per minute within the current firing sequence

For B rounds per burst and Q bursts per minute:
  internal gap = 60 / R
  final gap    = max(60 / R, 60 / Q − (B − 1) × 60 / R)

Firing TTK = sum of the BTK − 1 intervals before the lethal shot
First shot is at t = 0.

Analyzer model Four shots at 600 RPM take three intervals, or 300 ms. A one-shot kill has zero firing time, not zero projectile travel time. The accepted bolt/pump policy uses bolt time ÷ bolt speed + bolt delay + fire interval; DB-12 has a two-round pump cycle.

The UI can add resolved ADS time and one projectile flight time. Missing flight-model output makes flight-inclusive output unavailable; missing ADS time currently contributes zero. Those are implementation behaviors, not measured absence of delay.

Reload and selected magazine behavior

Analyzer model The tactical reload resolver has two distinct paths. An explicit animation override is already a magazine time and must not also be divided by the ordinary magazine reload factor.

Ordinary tactical reload:
  seconds = baseTacRld / (reloadTierFactor × ergoReloadMult)

Explicit animation override:
  seconds = tacRldOverrideMs / ergoReloadMult / 1000

Absent ergonomic multiplier = 1 (software neutral value)

PP-19’s 53-round magazine has an accepted 2667 ms override. With Improved Mag Catch at 1.063, the composed result is about 2508.937 ms and displays 2509 ms. That combined-loadout calculation is separate from a single-attachment panel observation.

Selected magData.mag replaces the base site capacity. Empty reload remains the weapon’s retained value; this tactical resolver does not derive attachment-adjusted empty reload or per-shell cycles. Invalid present inputs make timing unavailable rather than silently inventing a factor.

What the Analyzer computes

Level-flight model: dv/dt = −k v²

T(x) = expm1(k × x) / (k × v0)   for k > 0
T(x) = x / v0                    for k = 0

Two-dimensional trajectory (positive y upward):
  dp/dt  = v
  dvx/dt = −k × |v| × vx
  dvy/dt = gravity − k × |v| × vy

Analyzer model The level-flight expression supplies the optional TTK flight-time addition. The target view instead uses a vector RK4 solver with 1/500-s steps, an interpolated target-plane crossing and a 30-s limit. Zeroing searches a launch angle within −0.1 to +0.1 radians by 36 bisection iterations. Sight height is omitted.

Not established The native integration method, zeroing behavior, impact-time damage rule and all trajectory composition. Source coefficients agreeing with the model do not prove the game uses these equations.

Target plots are an additional model layer

Analyzer model Angular points project to distanceM × tan(angleDeg × π/180) × 100 centimetres. The site uses a 180-cm reference silhouette, an alpha threshold of 32/255 and approximate body-zone boundaries, not the game’s native hitboxes.

Pellet loads suppress target hit statistics because the plot has one direction per shell rather than one per pellet. A missing trajectory currently contributes zero vertical displacement in the view; that fallback is not evidence of no bullet drop.

Pre-shot spread and recovery

Analyzer model simulateSpread() starts at the selected state’s minimum, adds per-shot growth, applies separate firing/not-firing recovery between shots, then clamps to the state’s explicit minimum/maximum. Post-burst gaps can contain both recovery phases. Every weapon must define all four supported state pairs; there is no default maximum.

Analyzer model The current bare M433 software outputs are 0.36 ADS and 0.648 hip increase per shot. L1102’s exact ADS IncreasePerShot check is Float32 0.36000001430511475 at GS offset 736, bytes ec51b83e. These retain different precision for the source operand and the displayed explanation.

Minimum spread, per-shot increase, recovery and hard maximum are different inputs. A sustained-fire equilibrium or the UI’s one-magazine effective maximum is not the stored hard maximum, and it changes with cadence, magazine length and recovery.

Sources Weapons · Field Map · Recoil and spread model · L1102 M433 cross-check

The current radial sampling policy

r = spreadRadius × U ** distributionExponent
theta = 2π × V
x = centerX + r × cos(theta)
y = centerY + r × sin(theta)

U and V are independent uniform draws in [0, 1).

Analyzer model The current implementation consumes the source exponent: the usual 0.5 gives uniform area sampling; Interdictor’s moving ADS branch uses 0.67. Uniform radius sampling would use exponent 1 and would be more center-weighted. The source exponent is known; treating that exponent as this exact sampling law remains a model choice, not a decoded native algorithm.

The rendered shot is sampled around the pre-shot recoil center using the pre-shot spread radius. The same shot’s new recoil is delivered afterward; adding it before sampling would change the model.

Timed delivery with simultaneous recovery

Analyzer model genRecoilPts() returns pre-shot angular offsets. The first point is (0, 0). Each shot samples direction variation across the full interval, subtracts the chosen compensation vector, then delivers the remaining impulse uniformly over the selected duration while recovery acts at the same time.

Site recovery equation for each axis:

d(axis)/dt = deliveryRate
             − sign(axis) × (abs(axis) ** decExp + decOffset)
             × decFactor × t ** decTimeExp

t resets at every shot.
Unfinished earlier impulses continue; accumulated recoil is not erased.
Recovery clamps at zero.

Delivery integration uses steps at most 1 ms. The time-power integral is exact; displacement exponent 1 uses an exact recovery-step solution, while other displacement exponents use numerical steps. This is the site’s assumed continuous equation—not “constant recoil decay,” and not a recovered native solver.

Analyzer model All 126 audited base ADS/hip duration inputs are 0.025 s. A missing or zero duration currently falls back to 0.025 s in software. Other required recovery parameters do not receive a guessed substitute; their absence makes the dependent simulation unavailable.

Heat and additional source-only fields

Source mapping Heat inputs can be named without establishing an effective cooldown. The retained M27IAR own-wrapper control strengthens HeatPerBullet (Field_72a2b562) and HeatDropPerSecond (Field_e6120d22) under the qualified OverHeat (Field_43a8d9ab) owner. Overheat threshold, penalty and drop delay are separate operands.

Do not derive bullets-to-overheat or a sustained rate by dividing two numbers until the exact per-shot debit, timer, mode selection and recovery operation are known. In particular, transferring an infantry field name to a same-schema gadget is not a transfer of the entire infantry behavior.

Reproducing a source-to-site trace

StepRecordStop condition
1. Identify the selectionDisplay name, internal name, weapon/ammo/attachment choice and exact source build.A UI label or class category alone is not an identity join.
2. Resolve the ownersFull asset path, file/export GUIDs, actual declaring class and descriptor key.A same-hash alternate descriptor is not a matching control.
3. Read the selected memberNested path, primitive type, offset, bytes and raw-file SHA-256 from the receipt.Do not replace an own value with a registry default.
4. Find the data consumerGenerated JSON pointer and the exact sim/UI function reading it.Stored-but-unused data is not a simulated effect.
5. State the operationSource relation, accepted software equation and any test scope separately.No units, native order or availability claim without evidence.
6. Compare narrowlyPrecision-aware comparison at the relevant build and same selection.Earlier receipts carried forward by equal hashes are not new measurements.
