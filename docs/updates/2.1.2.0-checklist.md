## Decisions for this update

Proposed 9 November 2026; to be agreed with the operator before Stage 1.

1. **Use the gameplay-shape route list** for both the Stage 0 baseline and the post-update
   capture, so both sides match route for route. It becomes the new build's `raw-routes.txt`.
2. **Compare catalogs like with like** (fresh cache against fresh cache, or pass
   `--baseline-catalog` at a fresh-cache folder), never a loaded-cache catalog against a
   rebuilt one.
3. **Expect a new build, not a hotfix** (new map, new weapon, new gadget, new attachments,
   balance pass). The skill's decision step still decides.
4. **Ignore REDSEC** items in the notes.
5. **Never decode or open a `materialgrid_win32` file directly.** Use
   `scripts/frosty-material-grid-inventory.py`. The Mini Scout change is a damage-curve
   change (bullet file), not a grid change, unless the capture says otherwise.
6. **The NSW4 Carbine goes through "Adding a new weapon"** (guide Stage 9). The ADS-17
   6GHz Signal Jammer and the M142 MRAS are recorded for the gadget and vehicle notes only;
   they are not site work.
