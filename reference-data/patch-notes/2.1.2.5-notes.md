# Battlefield 6 Game Update 2.1.2.5 — patch notes

Source: Steam news, <https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2807960/view/680768104678031470>
(posted Wednesday 25 November 2026, 11:02 EST; download Thursday 26 November 09:00 UTC).
Read 26 November 2026. Text as EA wrote it; line numbers added for the Stage 7 note pass.

Game Update 2.1.2.5 rolls out across Battlefield 6 and REDSEC tomorrow, Thursday 26th,
delivering targeted improvements to the Supply Pouch, UI presentation, AI navigation, and
overall match stability.

## CHANGELOG

### PLAYER

1. General crash fixes across Battlefield 6 and REDSEC.

### GADGETS

2. Reduced the delay before firing a weapon after throwing a Supply Pouch.

### MAPS & MODES

3. Resolved an issue on European Casino where a flag could remain floating after its flagpole was destroyed.

### UI & HUD

4. Resolved an issue in the menus where Heist mode tiles displayed two subtitles.

### AI

5. Bots can now climb ladders as intended on Vegas Strip.

### REDSEC — UI & HUD

6. Supply Pouches now display their intended in-world icon.
