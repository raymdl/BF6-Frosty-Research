Note: passages come through WebFetch's summarising model, not a raw page dump; wording is as returned (quotes may be trimmed with "..." by the summariser). Source: EA's published update notes, 23 pages, versions ascending. Breakthrough-only passages are in breakthrough-passages-2026-10-19.md and are skipped here, except per-map layout passages (1.1.3.0 and 1.4.3.0) which are repeated under their maps. Searched modes: Conquest, Rush, Escalation, Domination, Team Deathmatch, Squad Deathmatch, King of the Hill, Strikepoint, Payload, Sabotage, Gauntlet, Battle Royale, Obliteration, Carrier Strike, Operations, Shutdown, Spooky Rush, Tank Hunt, Squad Shootout, Portal. Searched maps: Siege of Cairo, Empire State, Wake Island, Blackwell Fields, Iberian Offensive, Liberation Peak, Contaminated, Area 51, Manhattan Bridge, Eastwood, Operation Firestorm, Railway to Golmud, Tsuru Reef, Saints Quarter, New Sobek City, Vegas Strip, Cairo Bazaar, Hagental Base, Mirak Valley, Fort Lyndon. Not found in any update: Payload, Shutdown, Spooky Rush, Squad Shootout, Area 51, Vegas Strip, Blackwell Fields outside 1.2.1.0. "Tank Hunt" appears only as the REDSEC "Tank Hunter" mission. Portal and REDSEC Battle Royale/Gauntlet lines are included where the summariser returned them; very generic editor/UI fixes may have been dropped by the summariser. Only 1.4.3.0 and 1.1.3.0 gave full per-map rule numbers; most other notes contain no ticket/timer/round numbers.

### Carrier Strike
- "Added Out of Bounds volumes to reduce instances of spawn camping on the carrier deck."
- "Corrected an issue where HQ icons would not stick to the minimap in Rush and Breakthrough."
- "Fixed "Alliance Disbanded" notification incorrectly appearing during the final round of Gauntlet."
- "Corrected an issue where a player's class would be deselected after exiting a mode tutorial."
- "Jets will no longer spawn on the carriers once breached, and will only be available through the deploy screen."
- Developer comment: "We love seeing the battles on Carrier Strike, and we've also seen your feedback about spawn camping on carriers."
- Developer comment: "We're rolling out some adjustments to improve the overall experience of the mode."
- "Resolved an issue in Carrier Strike where the in-game Commander VO failed to play for the remainder of the round…"
### Custom Lobbies (modes list)
- "Expanded Custom Lobby creation support to cover:" Tactical Obliteration, Obliteration, Breakthrough, Escalation, Rush, Domination, Team Deathmatch, Squad Deathmatch, Custom Experiences.
- "Fixed an issue in Spectator mode on Conquest where the capture zone outline color did not match the correct team in some cases."
- "Corrected team color for Headquarters being incorrect in Conquest mode for spectators."
### Team Deathmatch
- "Added Breakthrough and Team Deathmatch score tickers." (Portal HUD)
### Portal
- "Portal and Custom Lobbies: Custom Lobby reliability, spectator tools, Spatial Editor functionality, Portal…"
- "Updated Portal's music system so queuing a new radio music track no longer restarts the currently playing track."
- "Added water plane visualization to Spatial Editor. This allows Portal creators to see the water level for levels."
- "Aircraft Carrier Variation mutator now has a new option to allow use of the Carrier Strike version."
- "Added Wake Island to SDK."
- "Players are now able to host or play Gulf of Lyndon: Night Ops Conquest without getting a "Timeout" error message"
- ViperStudiosAndy Conquest template: "Fixed Cairo Bazaar Combat Area", "New Map Added: Tsuru Reef"
- ViperStudiosAndy Breakthrough template: "New Map Added: Tsuru Reef"
### Battle Royale
- "We’re excited to bring Proximity Chat to Battlefield Battle Royale, but real-world testing will be…"
- "Opening the scoreboard in Battle Royale will show a list of recent players you’ve heard through Proximity Chat."
- "REDSEC Improvements: Battle Royale mission flow, ring interactions, redeployment, loot behaviour, spectating,…"
- "Corrected an issue where the Javelin launcher could not always be looted from the back of the Box Truck in Battle Royale."
- "Corrected an issue where the insertion helicopter player count could appear after the helicopter sequence had ended in Battle Royale."
- "A grace period has been added before a tank can be hunted by the Tank Hunter mission in Battle Royale."
- "Trackers consumed by the Ring of Fire now count towards Evasion mission progress in Battle Royale."
- "Corrected an issue where the Respawn Tower on the aircraft carrier had no visible outline when redeploying squad members in Battle Royale."
- "The system will account for spectating, waiting for redeploy in Battle Royale and similar situations." (Ranked)
### Fort Lyndon
- "Hunt for hidden intelligence, easter eggs and the larger mystery of what has caused the Fort Lyndon security network breach." (Phantom Program)
- "Improved the ability for players to see spotted enemies through objects after reaching Level 3 as Hazmat Breacher with the Tracker talent on Fort Lyndon."
- "AI bots in REDSEC can now use hanging ropes to reach the underground tunnels in Fort Lyndon."
### Iberian Offensive
- "Resolved an issue where some wall sections had no collision. Better wear a helmet when running into those now."
### Manhattan Bridge
- "Adjusted out-of-bounds on the first floor of a building near point E in conquest."
- "Adjusted several areas where the collision checks were overextended. This will make flying more predictable…"
### Tsuru Reef (Breakthrough layout, rule numbers)
- "Each sector has been expanded to allow both teams more flanking routes and the use of boats."
- Sector 1: "Added 2 RHIBs"; "Swapped Attacker Light Transport for APC"; "Expanded the size of the Capture Points at “A”"; "Moved “B” to a Warehouse along the Marina"
- Sector 2: "Increased the size of Capture volume B to include more cover"; "Swapped Attacker Light Transport for APC"; "Swapped Defender Light Transport for Quad"; "Moved Defender contested spawns back 10-15M"; "Fixed Attacker contested/non contested spawns at the beach"
- Sector 3: "Added an additional Capture Point down below across the waterway"; "Swapped Attacker APC for Light Transport"; "Removed APC for Defenders"; "Increased the size of Capture Point “A”"
- Sector 4: "Reduced the number of Capture Points from 2 to 1"; "Expanded the Capture Point size of “A” down at the Docks"
### Cairo Bazaar
- "Fixed Cairo Bazaar Combat Area" (Portal template, above)
